Remote sensing

Water Body Detection

Pixel60, 45
NDWI0.00
ClassLand

Maps surface water from Sentinel-2 and Landsat-8 imagery. Five classical models and two index baselines go head to head with a U-Net CNN on a spatially split test set.

U-Net: 0.984 water IoU on Sentinel-2, versus 0.950 for the classic NDWI threshold. On 30 m Landsat-8, gradient boosting edged it out.

Try it: switch views and hover the tile to read each pixel's water index.

  • PyTorch
  • U-Net
  • scikit-learn
  • Earth Engine
  • Rasterio

Learning to rank

Hotel Search Ranking

Ranks hotels in nearly 200,000 real Expedia searches so the ones travellers book appear first. Six rule-based rankings and two pointwise models go up against LambdaMART, scored on future searches with NDCG and offline A/B tests.

Booked hotel in the top 5 for 58.8% of searches, versus 42.4% for the best rule. Statistically level with Expedia's own production ranking.

  • LightGBM
  • LambdaMART
  • Feature engineering
  • A/B testing
  • pandas

Flags adenocarcinoma in H&E tissue slides. CLAHE contrast enhancement in OpenCV, then a fine-tuned ResNet.
